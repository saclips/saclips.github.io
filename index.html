About S.A. Clips

S.A. Clips publishes short, vertical highlight videos from gaming livestreams on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Our desktop tool turns livestream clips into vertical videos with subtitles and uploads them as drafts to our own TikTok account, where we review and publish them.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

1. Summary

S.A. Clips collects only the minimum data needed to upload videos to accounts you have authorized. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except the platforms you choose to post to.

2. Data we access from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, with your permission we access:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Upload permission (video.upload): used only to send videos you choose to your TikTok account as drafts.

We do not access your private messages, contacts, followers list, or any videos other than the ones the app uploads.

3. How we store data

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and other platforms are stored locally on the device that runs the app and are used only to perform the uploads you request.
  • The app keeps a local record of processed clips (clip link, title, creator name, upload status) to avoid processing or posting the same clip twice.
  • We do not operate a server that collects your personal data.

4. Data sharing

Videos and captions are sent only to the platforms you connect and choose to post to (for example TikTok, YouTube or Instagram). We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Once revoked, the stored token stops working.
  • To ask us to delete any data related to your account, email s.a.clips.work@gmail.com. We will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process data of children.
